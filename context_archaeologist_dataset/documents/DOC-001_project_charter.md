## Purpose
Northstar is Asterion Works' customer-facing workspace for consolidating service requests, account notes, and operational handoffs. The launch is intended for a limited cohort of existing business customers before a broader release.

## Baseline plan
The January planning baseline targeted a **20 April 2026** limited-cohort launch. That date was a planning target, not a contractual commitment. The product team expected the limited cohort to include approximately 40 invited customer accounts.

## Launch conditions
Before a cohort can be invited, the team needs:
- a release candidate accepted by Product;
- Security approval for the production configuration;
- a successful recovery rehearsal for the customer-data store;
- Support readiness for the first cohort.

The charter does not define the final approver for each condition. Those assignments are maintained in operational decision records.

## Scope boundaries
The initial cohort excludes automated billing changes and bulk import. The team may adjust the launch date as dependencies become clearer, but changes must be recorded in a decision record and reflected in the release plan.
