# Decision Record: Move Northstar Cohort Window

**Document ID:** DOC-003  
**Decision date:** 2026-02-18  
**Author:** Mira Sen, VP Product  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Superseded by DOC-008

## Decision
Move the limited-cohort target from **20 April to 11 May 2026**. This is a planning change, not authorization to launch on that date regardless of readiness.

## Rationale
The backup-policy rollout and recovery rehearsal were taking longer than forecast. Support also needed a stable week to complete its rota and customer communications.

## Conditions unchanged
The four launch conditions in DOC-001 remain in force. This decision does not waive Security approval or the recovery rehearsal.

## Follow-up
Program Management should update planning artifacts. If a later gate decision changes the date again, this record remains the evidence for the February decision only.
